# Research

Things that inspire me, influence my design, or that I want to learn more about
while building Indigo.

## Kakoune design

https://github.com/mawww/kakoune/blob/master/doc/design.asciidoc

Kakoune is orthogonal and composable, both in its feature design and its place
in a broader Unix environment. Similar to Jujutsu and Git, I find Kakoune to be
simultaneously simpler and more powerful than Vim.

## CodeMirror architecture

https://codemirror.net/docs/guide

https://marijnhaverbeke.nl/blog/facets.html

CodeMirror 6 embraces immutability and a "functional core, imperative shell"
architecture. Changes are transactional, extensions are composable without being
coupled.

## Xi

https://xi-editor.io/docs.html

Xi was overly ambitious and failed to meet its lofty goals, but its exploration
of ropes, CRDTs, and async plugins is interesting.

## Things

https://culturedcode.com/things/

Cultured Code's Things is a masterpiece. Its user interface is beautiful to look
at and satisfying to interact with. Its feature set is opinionated; the people
behind this have a vision and are disciplined in realizing it. Excellent German
engineering.

If I can make Indigo remotely as beautiful, satisfying, and effortless to use as
Things, I'll be very happy.
